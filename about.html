ABOUT EURO CERAMICS

A tile showroom
you can rely on.

Euro Ceramics is a ceramic and tile showroom located in Katuneriya, Sri Lanka.

We offer a range of ceramic and porcelain tile products for homes and businesses, with a focus on local accessibility, easy product enquiries and a convenient showroom experience.
